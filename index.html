How to play




  • W A S D move · Shift run · C sneak · Space jump
  • Mouse look · Click attack (the first click locks the mouse)
  • 1 / 2 / 3 sword, pistol, shotgun · R reload
  • F hold to guard with the sword · Q dodge roll · T lock on · G toss a stone to lure guards
  • E talk, open, pick up · V first or third person
  • M map · J journal · H help · F5 save · F9 load · Esc or Tab menu


  • Left stick moves (push it all the way to run)
  • Drag the right side of the screen to look around
  • ⚔ attack (hold to keep firing) · ⤒ jump · ⟲ dodge
  • USE talk, open, pick up · ↻ reload · ⇄ switch weapon
  • Top right: 👁 first or third person · ⤓ sneak · 🛡 hold to guard · 🗺 map · 🎯 lock on · Toss lure guards with a stone
  • ☰ Menu pauses, saves and opens settings. Turn your phone sideways for the best view.


Your goal
  1. Speak to Elder Maren in the village.
  2. Take the Iron Key from the shrine in the Emerald Wilds.
  3. Unlock the Iron Citadel gate and defeat the Warden.
  4. Cross the Hollow Throne: jump the moving platforms, then push the stone block onto the glowing plate.
  5. Defeat the Echo.


Tips
  • Sneak up behind unaware guards for a big sword hit. Running and gunfire are loud; walls block sight.
  • Armored brutes take far more damage from behind. Head shots do double damage; moving, running and jumping make guns less accurate.
  • Tap guard just before a hit lands to parry and stun the attacker. Torchlight makes you easier to spot, and guards notice bodies.
  • Optional: the Sunken Temple riddle (a shotgun), the Capital relic, the alarm terminal and three lore tablets.
  • Z drinks a healing potion (the test-tube button on phones). Talk to the villagers for side quests: a bounty, a delivery, herbs, a race and a smith.
  • Smash crates for loot. Maren resupplies you for 15 coins. The red diamond on the map is your objective.
